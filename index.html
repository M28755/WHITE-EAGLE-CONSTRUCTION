01 / Our Story

Built on ambition. Driven by a passion for construction.

White Eagle Construction began with a simple idea shared by two university students at Kirinyaga University pursuing studies in construction. With a common interest in building, design, and creating lasting structures, they saw an opportunity to turn what they were learning into something meaningful beyond the classroom.

What started as conversations about their future in the construction industry grew into a shared ambition to establish White Eagle Construction — a company built around quality, reliability, creativity, and a commitment to doing things right. Their journey reflects the belief that great things can begin with a small idea, a shared vision, and the determination to make it happen.

"We believe every great structure begins with a vision, grows through teamwork, and stands through quality workmanship."
